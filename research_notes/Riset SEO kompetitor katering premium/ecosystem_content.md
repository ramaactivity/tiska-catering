## 3. Google Business Profile and the local pack

### Takeaway
Google's official local ranking factors are relevance, distance and prominence. Reviews, ratings and complete information help, and ranking can't be bought. For a caterer, the key policy constraints are: few specific categories, a service area of at most ~2 hours' drive, a business name without keywords, and review requests that are neither incentivised nor gated.

### Cited Findings
- Local ranking = relevance, distance, prominence. "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." Replying to reviews "shows that you value their feedback". There is "no way to request or pay for a better local ranking". — [Google Business Profile Help: local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091)
- Categories: use "as few categories as possible to describe your overall core business". Choose categories that answer "this business IS a", not "HAS a". Choose the most specific category, and Google adds related general ones automatically. — [GBP guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)
- Service-area businesses: hide the address if customers aren't served at the location. The service area shouldn't extend "farther than about 2 hours of driving time" from the base. — [GBP guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)
- Business name: no taglines, keywords, locations or phone numbers. The name must match how it appears in the real world. — [GBP guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)
- Review policy: no incentives (payment, discounts, free goods/services). Don't "selectively solicit positive reviews" (review gating). Don't ask for "specific content". Asking genuine customers for reviews without incentives is allowed. — [Google Maps contribution policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114)

### Inferences
- Primary category "Caterer", plus perhaps "Wedding service" if Tiska really runs wedding packages (IS-a test). Don't pile on "Restaurant" unless there is a dine-in restaurant. PERKAWIS listing Tiska as "Restoran" is a sign of category inconsistency across the web that should be tidied.
- Tiska serves its location at Jl. Julang (tasting/office). If customers are received there, keep the address visible. Add the service area Bogor → Jakarta → Depok/Tangerang/Bekasi, which fits within ~2 hours.
- Premium-appropriate review requests: a personal note or WhatsApp from Ida after the event (with a direct review link) to **every** client, not just the happy ones, and no incentives. Replies should be gracious, and should name the couple or event only with permission. Photos of real events uploaded regularly, and Posts that mirror Kabar, add fresh signals.

### Gaps
- Typical rating and review counts for top premium caterers in Jakarta/Bogor (Akasya, Alfabet, Puspa, Kenanga, Adelia) on Google Maps could not be pulled (Maps can't be fetched). Check manually.
- No primary source was found quantifying the effect of photos or Posts on local pack ranking. Google only states that complete information helps.

## 4. Content strategies for premium event/wedding vendors

### Takeaway
Premium caterers abroad build content around venues (venue partners/guides), real events and cultural menus. Indonesian competitors mostly produce price-driven listicles. Tiska has room for elegant, useful content: real-wedding stories, a traditional-menu guide by ceremony (akad, siraman, lamaran, adat Sunda/Jawa/Betawi/Minang), and venue guides for Bogor/Jakarta.

### Cited Findings
- Purple Sage (Singapore, since 2002) organises its site by service (wedding catering, western sit-down, Chinese banquet, buffet, boutique butler, cocktail reception, luxury bentos). Its "Venue Partners" page shows 6 iconic venues (Asian Civilisations Museum, Gardens by the Bay, National Gallery, Jewel Changi, and others) with a "Send your request" CTA plus WhatsApp, and invites venues to become partners. Its wedding page stresses "personalised menus" that "reflect … cultural traditions". — [Purple Sage venue partners](https://purplesage.com.sg/venue-partners/); [Purple Sage wedding catering](https://purplesage.com.sg/wedding-catering/)
- Purple Sage keeps a separate testimonials page and a page linked to its SingaporeBrides feature. Both are social-proof content assets. — [Purple Sage testimonials](https://www.purplesage.com.sg/our-testimonials/); [Purple Sage x SingaporeBrides](https://www.purplesage.com.sg/wedding-catering-singaporebrides/)
- Indonesian competitors run blogs as SEO channels: listicles, "harga catering 2026" articles, and "murah mulai 20 ribu" landing pages by city. — [cateringprasmanan.com](https://www.cateringprasmanan.com/catering-di-bogor/); [catering.jagarasa.id](https://catering.jagarasa.id/catering-bogor-2025/); [dailycatering-bogor blog](https://dailycatering-bogor.com/blog/ini-daftar-10-catering-top-di-jabodetabek-untuk-pernikahan-kamu)
- Google, 15 May 2026: structured data is not required for AI Overviews/AI Mode, and there is no special markup for them. That means content quality, not schema, drives AI visibility. — [SEJ via search summary](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-drops-faq-rich-results-from-search/574429/); [Passionfruit](https://www.getpassionfruit.com/blog/what-changed-with-google-drops-faq-rich-results-and-what-to-do-now)

### Inferences
- Candidate content formats, elegant and with search demand:
  1. **Real Celebrations / Kisah Perayaan**: one page per event (couple/family and venue named with permission, menu served, large photos). These are long-tail pages for "[venue] + catering" and fit "customer as spotlight" exactly.
  2. **Venue guides**: "Catering di [venue] Bogor/Jakarta", covering venues Tiska has served (capacity, kitchen access, notes). This mirrors Purple Sage's venue partners and gives venues a reason to link back (venue backlinks and preferred-vendor lists).
  3. **Menu by tradition and ceremony**: explainers on adat Sunda/Jawa/Betawi/Minang menus and on akad, siraman, lamaran, pengajian. These are informational queries with no premium competitor.
  4. **Seasonal**: Ramadan/Lebaran hampers and year-end corporate. Kabar is the right place.
  5. **Bilingual (EN) pages**: only for pages with expat/corporate demand (corporate catering, wedding). Implement with hreflang. This is optional and should wait for demand data.
- Avoid "harga catering murah" pages that clash with the brand. Offering "mulai dari" pricing or a price guide may still be worth considering, because listicles consistently show prices (Rp/pax) and couples look for them. This is a brand decision for Rama.

### Gaps
- Rhubarb London (connection refused), Create Catering, Stamford and Neo Garden sites were not fetched. Their content patterns aren't verified here.
- No data on the organic traffic of any competitor's content format (would need Ahrefs/Semrush).

## 5. Technical: structured data and rich results 2026

### Takeaway
There is no "Caterer" type in schema.org, so keep LocalBusiness/FoodEstablishment (or plain LocalBusiness plus a Service offering). FAQ rich results were **fully deprecated on 7 May 2026**, so FAQPage markup no longer produces a SERP display but is harmless. Star ratings from reviews on Tiska's own site are not eligible (the self-serving rule).

### Cited Findings
- FoodEstablishment subtypes are only Bakery, BarOrPub, Brewery, CafeOrCoffeeShop, Distillery, FastFoodRestaurant, IceCreamShop, Restaurant and Winery. There is **no Caterer/CateringService type**. Relevant properties: acceptsReservations, hasMenu, servesCuisine, starRating. — [schema.org FoodEstablishment](https://schema.org/FoodEstablishment)
- Google LocalBusiness structured data: required `name` and `address`. Recommended: telephone, url, openingHoursSpecification, geo (≥5 decimals), priceRange (<100 characters), servesCuisine, menu, image (16x9, 4x3, 1x1), review/aggregateRating (for sites that collect third-party reviews). Use "the most specific LocalBusiness sub-type possible". — [Google Search Central: Local business](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business)
- Self-serving reviews: "If the entity that's being reviewed controls the reviews about itself, their pages that use LocalBusiness or any other type of Organization structured data are ineligible for star review feature". This includes embedded Google/Facebook review widgets. — [Google Search Central: Review snippet](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)
- FAQ rich results: restricted in Aug 2023 to "well-known, authoritative government and health websites" (HowTo was restricted at the same time), then deprecated entirely on 7 May 2026. The report and the Rich Results Test drop FAQ support in June 2026, and the Search Console API in August 2026. The markup can stay: "unused structured data doesn't cause problems for Search". — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-drops-faq-rich-results-from-search/574429/)

### Inferences
- Current Tiska setup (LocalBusiness/FoodEstablishment + FAQPage): keep it. Don't add aggregateRating from testimonials on the site, because it's ineligible and at risk of a manual action for misleading markup. Visible FAQs remain useful for users and AI answers even without a rich result.
- Add `areaServed` (Kota Bogor, Kabupaten Bogor, Jakarta, Depok, Tangerang, Bekasi), `hasMenu` pointing to /menu, `priceRange`, `sameAs` (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Bridestory) and `geo`. Optionally add a `Service` / `hasOfferCatalog` for Wedding, Corporate and Private events.
- Event schema is for public events with a date and location. It doesn't fit clients' private weddings. It only makes sense for public events such as Open Table, if they are open to the public (not verified against Google docs in this session).
- Image SEO: real-event photos with descriptive filenames and alt text (venue, menu), served through next/image. Google recommends multiple aspect ratios for LocalBusiness `image`.

### Gaps
- Google's Event rich result guidelines and image SEO docs were not fetched this session. The Event and image points above are inferences from general knowledge.
- No primary source was checked on whether Google plans more structured-data deprecations after May 2026 beyond FAQ.
